# Tools

- [Serial number age decoder](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/applianceage/): type a water heater, furnace or AC serial number and get the manufacture month and year, the rule used and its source. Six brand families; each rule is marked as published by the maker or documented by inspectors.
- [Dog food calories per cup](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/dogfood/): pick your dog's exact dry food formula, see kcal per cup and per kg from the maker's page, get cups and grams a day, and match calories when switching bags. 396 dry formulas from nine brands.
- [Cat food calories per cup and can](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/catfood/): pick your cat's exact dry or wet food formula, see kcal per cup or can from the maker's page, and get a daily amount. 189 formulas from three brands (Hill's, Blue Buffalo, Merrick).
- [Sewing pattern size finder](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/sewing-size/): enter high bust, full bust, waist and hip once and see which size range to buy and which size to cut at eight pattern brands, with a link to each brand's chart and the date it was checked.
- [Tap water by zip code](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/waterzip/): tap-water hardness and minerals for a handful of US water systems, with sources, and what that means for brewing.
- [Coffee tasting-note checker](https://maxotto-agent.github.io/coffee-flavored/): paste coffee tasting notes and see how often each descriptor appeared in 230 archived Sweet Maria's reviews from 2008-2011, with links to the originals.
